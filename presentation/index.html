PROBLEM STATEMENT

The Critical Flaw of Reactive Safety Apps

🚨 The Manual Action Fallacy
Most safety apps assume the victim can recognize danger and act in the moment (unlock phone, open app, tap panic button). Under real panic, physical restraint, or sudden threat, this assumption collapses.
🤐 The Coercion Blindspot
Attackers routinely force victims to unlock phones and reply "I'm fine." Traditional check-ins treat any reassuring response as safety confirmation and stand down immediately.
⏱️ Hesitation & False-Alarm Fear
Users hesitate to press loud panic buttons due to social embarrassment or fear of overreacting — delaying emergency assistance until danger is already unavoidable.
🔋 Always-On Surveillance Fatigue
Constant 24/7 background tracking drains phone battery and induces surveillance discomfort on both sides, leading users to disable safety features altogether.
CORE PHILOSOPHY

Inverting the Safety Model: Default Toward Caution

Traditional Safety Paradigm
Assumes Safe Until Explicit Panic
  • Passive idle state: App sits dormant waiting for user action.
  • Manual burden: Requires unlocking phone mid-crisis.
  • Naive verification: Any tap or "I'm okay" response stops alerts.
  • Outcome: Fails completely if user is restrained, incapacitated, or coerced.
Aurora Safety Paradigm
Assumes Risk Upon Anomalies; Proof Required
  • Continuous telemetry: Monitors speed, stillness, time, zones, battery.
  • Multi-factor compounding: Correlated anomalies trigger quiet check-in.
  • Strict safe-word stand-down: Only private authenticated phrase cancels.
  • Fail-closed default: Silence or wrong word escalates immediately to SOS.

VERIFICATION PROTOCOL

Fail-Closed Anti-Coercion Check-In

KEY INSIGHT: Coerced Confirmations Are Inherently Untrustworthy
In active danger, an assailant can easily force a victim to tap "I'm Safe" or type "Everything is fine." Aurora rejects unauthenticated reassurance: only the user's secret arbitrary safe phrase cancels escalation.
1. Elevated Trigger
When composite risk crosses into Elevated/High tier, Aurora suppresses alarms and launches a discreet prompt without alerting nearby assailants.
2. Secret Safe Word
Only the user's private phrase stands down the system. Typing "I'm okay" or any wrong word triggers immediate Emergency SOS dispatch.
3. Silence Escalation
Silence gives 3 timed retry cycles (30s window + 10s gap). If unanswered after 3 attempts, Emergency SOS automatically arms.
PRIVACY ARCHITECTURE

Session-Scoped Sharing vs Always-On Tracking

👤 Solo Mode
Zero Sharing by Default
Built for total privacy. All risk calculations, sensors, and state remain 100% on-device. Nothing is broadcast to external contacts unless the user triggers local SOS.
👥 Connected Mode
Emergency-Only Circle
Configures 1 to 3 trusted contacts. Circle is never notified during normal safe commutes — only alerted upon Critical tier transitions or Emergency SOS dispatches.
📍 Live Journey Share
Time-Boxed Active Walk
User explicitly starts monitoring before a specific walk and it auto-terminates on arrival. Eliminates both battery drain and recipient notification fatigue.
MARKET COMPARISON

Genuinely Novel Differentiators vs Market Apps

Market Reference: Life360, Noonlight, bSafe, Safetipin rely on manual buttons or static pre-computed maps. None offer live behavioral fusion with anti-coercion.
1. Continuous Behavioral Fusion
Existing apps score an area once in advance; Aurora scores a dynamic live moment by compounding movement kinematics, diurnal time, geofencing, and battery telemetry.
2. Fail-Closed Anti-Coercion
Existing panic buttons treat any tap as safe; Aurora defaults to security — silence and false reassurance fail closed and immediately arm Emergency SOS.
3. Session-Scoped Privacy
Eliminates standing 24/7 tracking. Users activate Live Journey Sharing only when needed, avoiding notification fatigue and surveillance anxiety.
4. Modular Pure-Function Engine
Pure mathematical scoring engine decoupled from UI. Can swap risk "lenses" (medical falls, pregnancy, elder care) without rewriting app architecture.

LIMITATIONS

Intellectual Honesty: Known PoC Constraints

📡 Simulated Notification Delivery
Trusted Circle notifications are currently simulated in client UI. Production rollout requires dedicated SMS/Push gateways (Twilio, FCM) for off-device recipient alerting.
🚔 Direct Dispatch Integration
Emergency flow surfaces native tap-to-call links (112, 100 in India). Automated PSAP CAD dispatch requires governmental public safety partnerships.
🗺️ Area Crime Statistics
Route familiarity is currently derived from user-saved geofenced zones. Ingesting macro historical crime records (e.g. NCRB precinct datasets) remains future scope.
🛡️ Physical Coercion Ceiling
Software cannot physically stop an in-person assailant. Safe-word protocols significantly raise the barrier to spoofing, but have physical real-world limits.
